About Us

Welcome to Riverstead SMP, a family-friendly Vanilla+ Minecraft server operating under the JoyaniaMC network. JoyaniaMC is a Christian community of creators, builders, and developers who work
together to craft extraordinary projects. JoyaniaMC's roots trace back to 2022 on a Minecraft server named Minecove. There, Joyed assembled a team to build a medieval town while welcoming
new players into our growing community. When Minecove announced its closure in 2024, Joyed and Ash decided to establish JoyaniaMC as an independent Minecraft server, granting our community
total creative freedom. Shortly thereafter, Minecove reopened and JoyaniaMC began picking up momentum; however, hosting expenses forced the project to pause. After navigating more than five server attempts and over two years of challenges, JoyaniaMC officially launched Riverstead on November 12th. We have been proudly running and expanding the server ever since.
